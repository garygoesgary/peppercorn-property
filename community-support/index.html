Completion of Micallef St, Riverstone

Community Statement of Support

Dear Blacktown City Council,

I/we are residents and/or property owners within the Micallef Street precinct and wish to express our support for the completion of Micallef Street to its full width as originally intended.

The current half-width road creates several issues for the surrounding community, including: road safety, vehicle access, traffic flow, amenity of the neighbourhood and access to the adjoining the public park.

In my/our opinion, completing Micallef Street would benefit not only the residents who live directly opposite the unfinished section of road, but also the broader community that uses the street on a daily basis.

I/we respectfully request that Blacktown City Council allocate funding and prioritise the completion of this section of Micallef Street.
